# Welcome

A deck made of Markdown – one slide per section.

Use **→** or **Space** to go on, **F** for full screen.

---

## How it works

- Slides live in `slides.md`
- `---` on its own line starts a new slide
- **Edit** changes them right here (a new version each time)
- **Print** makes a PDF, one slide per page
